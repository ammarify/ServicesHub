# Security policy

## Supported versions

Fixes are made on the `main` branch.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities privately: open this repository's **Security**
tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Don't open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it and the impact you expect.
Reports are usually acknowledged within a few days, followed by a fix or a
plan once the issue is confirmed.

## How the app is secured

[servihub-api/SECURITY.md](servihub-api/SECURITY.md) describes sessions and
tokens, the permission matrix, how the customer simulator is scoped, the AI
guardrails and the known limitations.
